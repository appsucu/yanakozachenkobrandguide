# Publication Rules Specification

## Purpose
The publication rules capability provides a pre-flight quality checklist and aesthetic requirements to ensure consistency across published brand assets.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Quality assurance checklist
The system SHALL provide an interactive checklist covering color contrast verification, typography hierarchy consistency, and whitespace margins.

#### Scenario: Pre-flight checklist review
- **WHEN** creator consults the publication checklist before publishing content
- **THEN** concrete validation points for contrast, margins, font pairings, and logo clearance are provided

### Requirement: Tone of voice and aesthetic guidelines
The system SHALL specify brand identity principles governing image mood, emotional warmth, concise text expression, and visual unclutteredness.

#### Scenario: Checking aesthetic rules
- **WHEN** user reviews tone of voice guidelines
- **THEN** core aesthetic pillars emphasizing warmth, professional clarity, and intentional restraint are presented
